# Constraints

Each constraint records its source and consequence.

- **Preserve the selected template.** Source: owner initialization request.
  Keep its numbered folders, scripts, constitution, generated prompts, append-only
  governance, and manual /2 operating loop. Customize declared project fields.
- **Progressive context.** Source: owner fresh-session requirement. Enter through
  AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md, use per-folder CONTEXT.md, and declare bounded reads/writes.
  Do not load the whole repository, research, or unrelated inactive workspaces.
- **Non-Python product.** Source: owner preference. Python remains template
  workflow tooling only. Product language/toolchain is not silently selected.
- **Codex and Claude first.** Source: owner scope correction. Defer OMP/Cursor
  implementation and HarnessRouter integration until evidence warrants them.
- **Outcome-driven routing.** Source: owner requirements. Measure exact model +
  effort + difficulty + harness/tool configuration, not prose tier descriptions.
- **Full recovery accounting.** Source: owner cost lens. Include failed attempts,
  abandoned work, corrections, escalation, tool charges, and parent repair.
  Record reliability with cost; never label zero completed work a saving.
- **Wrong tool calls can fail an attempt.** Source: owner quality criterion.
  Preserve operational failure even after recovery. The proposed three-call
  threshold is provisional and still needs policy confirmation/calibration.
- **Cheap observation.** Source: owner cost requirement. Ordinary collection,
  counters, and start/end reconciliation make zero model calls. Budget judgment
  and benchmarking separately. Continuous observation does not mean continuous
  LLM inference.
- **Crash-safe, attributable evidence.** Source: owner abrupt-session concern.
  Events need stable IDs, durable processing, idempotence, and actual caller
  attribution. Unknown outcome/model/cost remains unknown.
- **Bounded authority.** Source: owner global rules and template constitution.
  A route cannot broaden tools, permissions, credentials, or approval authority.
- **Private machine state stays local.** Source: owner/template safety rules.
  Do not commit secrets, resolved host paths, private transcripts, or raw session
  telemetry. Local runtime settings and bulky rebuildable output stay ignored.
- **Evidence before claims.** Source: research and owner verification rules.
  Model benchmarks are priors, not proof of tool competence or a crossover in
  this harness. Distinguish proposed, observed, measured, and runtime-confirmed.
- **Manual operation.** Source: template /2 compatibility boundary. No autonomous
  runner is qualified here; leave optional runner/front-repository tooling inert.
